Conversation With the SeaConversationwith the Sea

In Jerusalem, a court orders a 60-year-old man to repay a social-security debt said to be owed by his son, who drowned in the Red Sea decades ago and whose death no record shows.

A film by Muayad Alayan · Hiwar ma’ albahr · 2026

Kamel El Basha, who plays Kamal, 23 October 2021. Photo: Ahmed Nader Mohamed / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

Kamal is sixty and lives in Jerusalem. An Israeli court orders him to repay a social-security debt said to be owed by his son Wael, who drowned in the Red Sea decades ago, though no official record of the death can be found. Following the only lead left, Kamal goes looking for what happened, and finds the relatives and friends who have long held a grudge against him.

Muayad Alayan wrote the film with his brother Rami Alayan. He has said the opening incident comes from real events in his family, and that the rest is fiction drawn from life in Jerusalem, a love-and-hate letter to his home city. Kamel El Basha plays Kamal; Amer Hlehel plays the lawyer friend who helps him.

Muayad Alayan

A Jerusalem-based director and producer, he co-founded PalCine Productions in Bethlehem with Rami Alayan and is a member of the European Film Academy. This is his fourth film with MAD World handling sales.
