The Jerusalem Festival’s last scheduled day

Back on 16 August after a pause since 2023, the festival opened with the Jerusalem Arabic Music Ensemble’s tribute to Ziad Rahbani and ran two months of concerts, talks and exhibitions across East Jerusalem. All music stories

Everything the music desk has reported for this edition, newest first.

  1. 25Oct

    Nai Barghouti performs 'Music of Palestine' at HKUST's Shaw Auditorium in Hong Kong on 25 October.

    Singer and flautist Nai Barghouti plays the last listed concert of HKUST's Cosmopolis Festival, 'Music of Palestine' on 25 October 2026 at 19:30. Her ensemble mixes original songs with arrangements of classic Arabic repertoire, jazz and Western classical music. She also featured on MC Abdul's 2026 single 'Wait for Me'.

    Nai Barghouti, 18 October 2019. Photo: Ahotsa Info / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0

    Sources: Cosmopolis Festival, HKUST 15 Oct

  2. 24Oct

    Faraj Suleiman plays his first fully solo show, 'Faraj, Piano and Mic', in Edmonton on 24 October; a Ziad Rahbani tribute at the Barbican follows on 23 January 2027.

    Palestinian pianist and composer Faraj Suleiman performs 'Faraj, Piano and Mic' at the Horowitz Theatre in Edmonton on 24 October 2026, billed as his first completely solo show: one piano, one microphone, no band, in Arabic, with songs from 'Better than Berlin', 'Second Verse' and 'Maryam'. MARSM has also announced Suleiman leading a 20-piece ensemble and guest singers in a tribute to Ziad Rahbani at London's Barbican on 23 January 2027.

    Faraj Suleiman at WOMEX, 27 October 2019. Photo: WOMEX / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 2.0

    Sources: Showpass, Faraj Piano and Mic Edmonton 15 Oct, Scene Noise 20 Aug

  3. 16Oct

    Gaza-born rapper MC Abdul releases his debut album 'Day by Day, Shway Shway' on EMPIRE on 16 October.

    MC Abdul, 18 and now based in the Bay Area, releases his nine-track debut album 'Day by Day, Shway Shway' on 16 October 2026 through EMPIRE, with Suhel Nafar, head of EMPIRE's West Asia and North Africa division, as executive producer. Singles before the album included 'Wait for Me' (with Nai Barghouti), 'Wein Abdul?' and 'Shway Shway', produced by !llmind. He performs at Palestine Culture Day in Redwood City on 17 October.

    MC Abdul in Dallas, 28 September 2024. Photo: FeaturingDallas / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

    Sources: KQED 14 Oct, Apple Music, MC Abdul 15 Oct, 48 Hills 22 Sep

  4. 15Oct

    The 30th Jerusalem Festival, run by the Yabous Cultural Centre, reaches the scheduled end of its two-month run on 15 October.

    The Jerusalem Festival returned on 16 August 2026 at the Yabous Cultural Centre in East Jerusalem after a pause since summer 2023, opening with a tribute to Ziad Rahbani played by the Jerusalem Arabic Music Ensemble. AFP reported it would run two months, until 15 October, with concerts, talks and exhibitions at venues across East Jerusalem. Sharjah24 calls it the 30th edition. Yabous board chairman Suheil Khoury said the festival returns to give people hope.

    Damascus Gate, Jerusalem’s Old City, 8 November 2022. Photo: بدارين / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

    Sources: Yabous Cultural Centre, final night listing 22 Sep, Oman Observer 18 Aug, Sharjah24 17 Aug

  5. 10Oct

    Berlin's AL.Festival (2-10 October, Festsaal Kreuzberg) included oud player Kamilya Jubran and rapper Daboor.

    AL.Festival's 2026 edition ran from 2 to 10 October at Festsaal Kreuzberg in Berlin. Its announced line-up included Palestinian oud player and composer Kamilya Jubran, Palestinian rapper Daboor of BLTNM, Tunisian singer Badiaa Bouhrizi, Egyptian producer Abadir and a showcase from the Syrian Cassette Archives.

    Festsaal Kreuzberg, Berlin, 16 September 2023. Photo: Molgreen / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

    Sources: Scene Noise 28 Jul

  6. 5Oct

    The National's best Arabic songs of 2026 list includes Saint Levant and Tul8te's 'Nano', a No. 1 on Spotify Egypt.

    The National's 5 October list of the best Arabic songs of 2026 includes 'Nano' by Tul8te and Saint Levant (1 June), which it says reached No. 1 on Spotify in Egypt and stayed in the top 10 through the summer. It also picks 'Dawaween' by Jordanian rapper Shbash with Palestinian artist Rknddn (13 February) and 'Nassaba' by Jordanian-Palestinian singer Siilawy (17 April).

    Saint Levant in Madrid, 22 July 2025. Photo: Rastrojo / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

    Sources: The National 5 Oct

  7. 25Sep

    Rasha Nahas released the single 'Manaqish' on 25 September; her Meshell Ndegeocello-produced third album, billed for October, has no listed date yet.

    Haifa-born, Berlin-based Rasha Nahas released the single 'Manaqish' on 25 September 2026. HAU Hebbel am Ufer, which hosted her preview concert on 21 May, said her next album, produced by Meshell Ndegeocello, would be released in October; Wikipedia still lists it as forthcoming and Apple Music shows no pre-release. Apple Music lists a Berlin concert at Festsaal Kreuzberg on 14 November.

    Rasha Nahas at WOMEX, 27 October 2024. Photo: WOMEX / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 2.0

    Sources: Apple Music, Rasha Nahas 15 Oct, HAU Hebbel am Ufer 21 May

  8. 17Sep

    Together for Palestine says its benefit concert is 'coming to New York City soon'; no date, venue or line-up has been named.

    Together for Palestine announced plans for a New York fundraising concert on Instagram on 17 September 2026, a year after its Wembley Arena show of 17 September 2025, according to DJ Mag. The organisation's website says the event is 'coming to New York City soon' and offers ticket alerts, with no date, venue or line-up. The London concert featured Palestinian artists including Saint Levant, Elyanna, Nai Barghouti, Sama' Abdulhadi and Faraj Suleiman.

    Sources: Together for Palestine 15 Oct

  9. 2Sep

    Saint Levant moved the North American leg of his Afandi World Tour to spring 2027 over US visa checks, so this week's original Los Angeles and San Diego dates are off.

    Saint Levant (Marwan Abdelhamid) released his second album, 'Afandi', his first entirely in Arabic, on 21 August 2026. On 2 September he postponed the North American leg of the Afandi World Tour after his US visa, initially approved, was sent for additional background checks; the leg now opens in Ottawa on 19 March 2027, with Boston on 27 March as the first US date, and tickets are honoured or refunded. The original plan had him at The Wiltern, Los Angeles on 13-14 October and in San Diego on 15 October. The National still lists a 19 December show at Dopa World in Dubai.

    Saint Levant at Melbourne Town Hall, 4 June 2026. Photo: Matt Hrkac of Matt Hrkac Photography / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0

    Sources: The National 2 Sep, Arab News 3 Sep, The Music Universe 17 Jul
